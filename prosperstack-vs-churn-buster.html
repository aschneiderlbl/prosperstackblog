Both handle cancellation and recovery. Which fits your requirements?

ProsperStack and Churn Buster help subscription businesses respond to customers who cancel and recover payments from those who did not intend to leave. Both offer cancellation surveys, segmentation and offer testing.

The decision depends on what you need those tools to do: which customer data should influence the experience, how much of the flow you want to customize, how you measure offer performance and which billing actions your platform supports.

Choose Churn Buster if its cancellation tools meet your targeting and offer requirements and failed-payment recovery is a central part of your evaluation. Choose ProsperStack if you need native CRM/CDP connections, configurable cancellation experiences and offer optimization that can account for discount cost, margin and expected future payments.

This comparison looks at where the products overlap, the differences that can change your decision and the package you will need to use them.

When is Churn Buster the right fit?

Churn Buster is a credible option for businesses evaluating cancellation prevention and failed-payment recovery together. Its Cancel Flows product captures exit reasons, segments customers, supports save offers and tests their performance. Sentiment AI analyzes free-text feedback, and repeat-offer cooldowns limit how often a subscriber can accept the same incentive.

That may cover your cancellation requirements if your strategy relies on billing attributes such as plan, product or tenure and familiar interventions such as pauses, skips, discounts and plan changes. Confirm the actions available on your billing platform.

There is no reason to switch simply to add a cancellation survey or start testing offers. The reason to evaluate an alternative is a requirement your current setup does not cover well. For ProsperStack, the areas to examine are connected customer data, experience controls and offer economics.

What customer data should change the cancellation experience?

Both products support segmentation. Churn Buster describes targeting by plan, product and tenure. ProsperStack connects billing and custom subscriber data with native CRM/CDP integrations such as Salesforce, HubSpot, Klaviyo and Segment.

Consider two subscribers selecting “too expensive.” One has an unresolved support issue recorded in your CRM; the other has used the product regularly for a year. You may want to offer help to the first and a targeted incentive to the second. This is an illustrative workflow.

If data outside your billing platform should change the intervention, ProsperStack's native connections are a specific reason to evaluate it. Bring the same fields and targeting rules to a Churn Buster demo so you can compare the implementation required. A generic segmentation checkbox will not answer that question.

How much control do you need over the cancellation flow?

Both products can ask why a customer is leaving and present an alternative. Churn Buster combines surveys and save offers with testing and reporting. ProsperStack's cancellation platform lets you adapt messaging, questions, offers and flow experiences to subscriber context.

The useful comparison is a complete customer journey. For a subscriber with a support problem, can you show a relevant help option? For one who no longer needs the current plan, can you offer a supported plan change? Can eligibility rules prevent an inappropriate discount?

Build the same example in both products and compare the controls, data inputs and billing actions involved. If Churn Buster covers the experience you need, customization alone is not a reason to move. ProsperStack becomes worth evaluating when your requirements involve connected customer context and several distinct routes or interventions.

What makes a retention offer successful?

Both products automate offer optimization. Churn Buster Smart Offers prioritizes variants with the highest save rate. ProsperStack's Autopilot can optimize acceptance or forecasted value, factoring in discount cost, gross margin and expected post-offer payments.

Autopilot also generates and tests incentives and offer copy within configured discount and duration limits. Your team reviews generated copy before it goes live.

Suppose a larger discount saves more customers than a smaller one. The extra acceptances are useful, but you still need to understand whether they justify the discount and whether those subscribers keep paying. Forecasted-value optimization gives your team a way to include those economics in offer selection.

If maximizing acceptance is your objective, compare the testing and allocation tools in both products. If margin and expected subsequent payments should guide selection, evaluate Autopilot's forecasted-value objective. Check actual payments as cohorts mature: a forecast is a decision input, not proof of retained revenue.

How does payment recovery fit your existing billing setup?

Churn Buster's Dunning combines recovery outreach with retry coordination and monitoring. Setup can involve disabling existing failed-payment emails and adjusting retry schedules on some platforms. Confirm who controls retries on yours.

ProsperStack's Revenue Recovery provides segmented communications, secure payment updates and personalized recovery offers alongside your billing platform's retries. It does not replace the retry engine.

If failed-payment recovery is the main problem you are trying to solve, give Churn Buster's Dunning a direct evaluation. If retries already work well and you need targeted outreach connected to cancellation and reactivation workflows, examine ProsperStack's recovery campaigns.

Compare results using the same baseline, eligible payments and completed cohorts. Ask each provider to identify which parts of your current recovery setup it would change and how you would measure the effect.

Will the right billing actions work on your platform?

A platform logo does not tell you which product or action is supported. Churn Buster publishes different platform coverage for Dunning and Cancel Flows. ProsperStack connects to SaaS, ecommerce and publishing billing systems, including Stripe, Recharge, Naviga and Piano, with feature support varying by integration.

Before choosing, ask both providers to demonstrate your required pause, skip, coupon, plan-change and payment-update actions. Include data synchronization and any custom integration work in the evaluation.

What can your AI assistant do?

Churn Buster's MCP provides read-only access to Dunning and Cancel Flows data and is included on every plan, including Measure. It helps an assistant analyze results without changing configuration.

ProsperStack supports analysis and changes to retention resources through MCP, with your oversight. MCP is included on Prosper and Enterprise. Read-only access suits analysis; write access is useful when you also want your assistant to help implement approved changes. See ProsperStack's MCP capabilities.

What support and implementation help will you need?

Churn Buster includes hands-on help with setup and ongoing reviews. Its Dunning service describes a 45-day review, maintenance and monitoring. That can be valuable when your team wants help diagnosing recovery issues or keeping tests moving.

ProsperStack includes email support on every plan. Enterprise adds guided onboarding, integration assistance, dedicated developer support and one-on-one retention strategy sessions. Compare support by plan.

Include your data connections, billing actions and existing flows in the implementation discussion. Confirm what the provider will configure, what your team will maintain and what additional work the quote covers.

Compare the package you will use, not just the starting price

Churn Buster's published pricing starts at $269/month for Dunning or Cancel Flows individually and $430/month for both. Fees are set by MRR tier, with month-to-month plans and no commission on recoveries.

ProsperStack Grow starts at $200/month. For A/B testing, Autopilot, CRM/CDP integrations and MCP, compare Prosper, starting at $750/month, or Enterprise. Cancellation pricing depends on monthly session volume.

These starting prices cover different packages. Request quotes using the same cancellation volume, MRR, required products and support needs. An MRR-based fee and a session-based fee can change differently as your business grows.

Which should you choose?

Choose Churn Buster when:

  • You want to evaluate cancellation prevention and failed-payment recovery together.
  • Its segmentation, save offers and supported billing actions cover your cancellation strategy.
  • Save-rate optimization matches your offer objective.
  • You value hands-on help with implementation and ongoing reviews.

Choose ProsperStack when:

  • CRM/CDP or custom customer attributes should shape cancellation experiences.
  • You need to configure different questions, routes, offers and deflections for customer segments.
  • Discount cost, margin and expected future payments should inform offer optimization.
  • You want cancellation, recovery and reactivation workflows connected in one retention platform.
  • Your AI assistant should help implement approved changes as well as analyze results.

Start with a workflow you need to improve and compare how each product handles it. The strongest reason to choose ProsperStack is a concrete requirement it can meet—not the fact that it offers another cancellation flow.

Questions to resolve before switching

Can ProsperStack replace Churn Buster's Dunning?

ProsperStack can provide recovery communications, payment updates and incentives, but does not replace billing-platform retries. Map the retry configuration, outreach and monitoring you currently use before deciding whether it covers your recovery requirements.

Does moving cancellation flows mean moving recovery too?

Scope the two workflows separately. Confirm with both providers how events, billing actions and communications will interact if you retain one recovery service while changing cancellation tools. Include that coordination in your implementation plan.

How should we validate a migration?

Document existing offers, eligibility rules, data fields and billing actions. Test representative subscriber scenarios, agree on the cutover and check that recovery emails do not overlap. Preserve a baseline so you can assess results after the change.
