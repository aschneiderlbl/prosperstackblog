Two approaches to reducing subscription churn

Subscription businesses lose customers in two ways: some choose to cancel, while others leave because a payment fails. ProsperStack and Churn Buster address both, but their strengths differ. Churn Buster emphasizes failed-payment recovery. ProsperStack emphasizes using customer data to personalize cancellation experiences and improve the value of retained subscriptions.

The right fit depends on where you need the most help:

Choose ProsperStack when you want to personalize cancellation flows with CRM/CDP data, optimize offers for long-term revenue and profit, and bring back canceled subscribers or convert free users to paid.

Choose Churn Buster when failed-payment recovery is your main priority and you want dedicated dunning alongside cancellation flows.

Why ProsperStack’s cancellation flows go deeper

Both products put a flow in front of the cancel button, ask why, and present a save offer. The differences are in how much of that flow your team controls, what data decides who sees what, and what happens next.

Design conditional questions and steps

Churn Buster’s documented flow starts with deflections, followed by an exit survey and targeted offers. ProsperStack’s flow editor is no-code and lets you add conditional survey questions and steps, branch on earlier answers, include an instant-cancel step, and apply custom CSS. A subscription box brand and a B2B SaaS team can build very different flows in the same tool.

Targeting uses more than billing data

In Churn Buster, its team builds segments with you, and what you can target depends on what your subscription platform sends. Attributes like customer tags may need a custom property added to the snippet. In ProsperStack, segments can draw on billing data, custom properties, Salesforce fields, Segment traits and existing Klaviyo segments, and your team builds them directly. Offer rules can also limit an offer to once per customer, helping prevent repeated discounts to the same subscriber. Traffic-percentage limits let your team control how widely an offer is shown.

Personalize within one flow

Churn Buster segments by sending each customer type into its own complete flow. Its docs note that every split divides your traffic, so smaller segments take longer to show which offer works. ProsperStack supports separate flows too, but can also vary experiences and offers by segment within a single flow. Your team can manage those variations without maintaining a separate flow for every segment.

Test flows and optimize offers for forecasted value

Churn Buster’s Smart Offers rotate variations of a targeted offer and shift traffic toward the best save rate. ProsperStack tests at both levels. It runs A/B tests on entire flows, and Autopilot tests offers, generates copy for your approval, and can optimize for forecasted value instead of acceptance. In its published case study, the Minnesota Star Tribune ran 15 A/B testing sequences in its first year and achieved an 18.5% online save rate. A published account reported an 8.8% call-center save rate. These are results from different channels, rather than a controlled comparison with Churn Buster.

The flow connects to what happens after

When a subscriber cancels anyway, ProsperStack can automatically start a winback campaign. Survey answers can be written back to Salesforce; survey responses and session status can be sent to Klaviyo, so other teams can act on the reasons. Churn Buster provides reporting and data exports; ProsperStack’s distinction is its documented native sync to Salesforce and Klaviyo.

Proven at volume

Hootsuite already had a cancellation flow. With ProsperStack, it used segmented offers and targeted deflections to prevent $997,000 in revenue churn and retain more than 18,000 customers in its first year. The case study shows how one team improved its existing cancellation program.

Where Churn Buster’s approach fits: its team builds and tunes the flow for you, which can suit a lean team that prefers a managed service. If you want your own team running a retention program on top of your customer data, ProsperStack offers more room to do it.

Which platform is the better fit?

Choose ProsperStack when:

  • You want Salesforce fields, Segment traits or Klaviyo segments to shape cancellation experiences.
  • You want to optimize offers for forecasted value, including discount cost, margin and expected future payments.
  • You need a documented connection for Chargebee, Paddle, Maxio or Piano.
  • You want winback campaigns, free-to-paid conversion incentives and a billing portal alongside cancellation flows.

Choose Churn Buster when:

  • Failed-payment recovery is your main priority.
  • You want dedicated dunning with retry coordination and customer outreach.
  • Its supported billing integrations and cancellation tools cover your requirements.

ProsperStack vs. Churn Buster FAQs

What is the main difference between ProsperStack and Churn Buster?

ProsperStack emphasizes CRM/CDP-driven cancellation experiences, offer optimization and lifecycle tools. Churn Buster emphasizes dedicated failed-payment recovery alongside cancellation flows. Both offer segmentation and save offers.

Can I use Salesforce, Segment and Klaviyo data in ProsperStack?

Yes. Salesforce contact fields, Segment Identify traits and Klaviyo profile properties can map to subscriber custom properties. Klaviyo also syncs existing segments. These connections support targeting and reporting and are available on Prosper and Enterprise.

Does ProsperStack send cancellation data back to my customer tools?

Salesforce can receive mapped cancellation survey answers. Klaviyo can receive survey responses and session statuses. Segment’s documented destination sends Identify traits into ProsperStack; data direction differs by integration.

How do Autopilot and Smart Offers differ?

Churn Buster Smart Offers prioritizes save rate. ProsperStack Autopilot can optimize acceptance or forecasted value, including discount cost, gross margin and expected post-offer payments. It also generates offer copy for review before publication.

Which billing integrations differ?

ProsperStack lists connections for Paddle, Maxio and Piano that Churn Buster does not list. Churn Buster lists connections for Loop, Skio, Smartrr and Orb that ProsperStack does not list. Compare ProsperStack’s integrations with Churn Buster’s supported platforms, and confirm coverage for the product and billing actions you need: Dunning and Cancel Flows support different platforms. Custom API connections are separate from listed integrations.

Do both products support Shopify subscription businesses?

Yes. ProsperStack connects through supported subscription apps. Churn Buster documents Shopify via a custom app and integrations with subscription apps. Check product coverage and supported billing actions for your setup.

Does ProsperStack replace my billing platform’s payment retries?

No. Your subscription platform handles retries. ProsperStack adds targeted recovery communications, secure payment-update links and incentives. Churn Buster is worth evaluating when dedicated failed-payment recovery is your main requirement.

Does ProsperStack offer tools beyond cancellation flows?

Yes. ProsperStack offers winback campaigns, free-to-paid conversion incentives and a self-service billing portal. Churn Buster’s published product lineup centers on Dunning and Cancel Flows.
