Similar features, different approaches to retention

ProsperStack and Raaft are retention platforms that help subscription businesses reduce voluntary churn at the moment of cancellation with cancellation flows, targeted offers and exit feedback. They differ in emphasis.

Raaft provides a simple, self-serve cancellation flow for SaaS.

ProsperStack is designed for high-volume subscription businesses across SaaS, digital media and ecommerce that prioritize retention system depth and continuous improvement at scale.

ProsperStack vs. Raaft, at a glance

Both ProsperStack and Raaft are credible options for subscription businesses. The right choice depends on your industry, volume, where you want depth and how your team thinks about retention.

Consider Raaft if: You run a SaaS business and want a no-code cancellation flow live quickly, with a free plan to start. Raaft pairs conditional offers with exit feedback and churn reporting.

Choose ProsperStack if: You have high cancellation volume and want to systematically improve how you respond to churn. ProsperStack provides the customer context, conditional logic, experimentation, reporting and AI controls required to practice retention at the highest level.

Raaft emphasizes fast setup, self-service flow management and product feedback from churned users.

ProsperStack is organized around retention as an ongoing practice, helping businesses develop retention strategies, deliver tailored interventions, test hypotheses, measure outcomes and continuously improve retention performance.

The retention optimization loop

Cancellation is one of the highest leverage moments in the customer lifecycle, and the greater a business’s subscription churn volume, the greater the return on each improvement, big and small. Both platforms support customer segmentation and conditional offers, but ProsperStack’s experimentation, feature depth and customizability make it the best choice for high-volume brands.

1. Monitor churn with greater context

Understanding cancellation and retention behavior at scale requires context. Both platforms capture exit reasons and subscription data, and Raaft breaks feedback down by flow, reason, time period and plan. ProsperStack adds CRM/CDP properties and segments through native integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, Klaviyo and others, providing you a clearer view of who is leaving, what they have in common and which churn patterns merit attention.

Additionally, connect your AI agent via MCP to analyze retention performance, suggest improvements and build them into your flow under your supervision.

2. Create tailored interventions with more flexibility

Survey questions, offers and deflections are the main retention drivers at the cancellation moment. Raaft connects each survey answer to an offer (discount, pause, plan switch, custom link or call back) and selects flows by audience rules such as plan. ProsperStack gives you greater depth of incentive and deflection options and allows you to conditionally adapt flow routes, steps, survey questions, offers, deflections, confirmation pages and eligibility rules based on customer segments, including custom and CRM/CDP data.

3. Test, measure and improve

Raaft does not document A/B testing; each subscriber sees the flow their audience matches. ProsperStack A/B tests flows and offers on Prosper and Enterprise plans, and weighted rules allow high-volume brands to validate changes on a smaller share of traffic before rolling out more broadly.

ProsperStack’s AI Autopilot accelerates offer optimization by generating offer variants within your economic guardrails, testing them with customers and shifting traffic to the best performers. By factoring in discount amount, gross margin and post-save payments, Autopilot can optimize for long-term value, not just save rate.

Dashboards and reporting in ProsperStack show you where you’re getting results, and where things could improve. Adding publication notes when saving a cancellation flow in ProsperStack allows you to see what effect each change had on performance.

Autopilot vs. Raaft conditional offers

ProsperStack uses AI to optimize offer performance. Raaft presents the offer you assign to each cancellation reason.

ProsperStack Autopilot

With Autopilot, you can:

  • Incorporate gross margin and expected post-offer payments to optimize for forecasted value, not just acceptance rate
  • Define discount, duration and maximum generosity boundaries
  • Generate and test offer headlines, body copy and button text
  • Review, edit or regenerate AI-created copy before it goes live
  • Compare Autopilot with a control

Autopilot handles ongoing variant generation, testing and allocation within those boundaries. The retention team still defines the objective and controls what the system is allowed to do.

Raaft conditional offers

Raaft presents dynamic offers based on plan, churn reason or other criteria. Offers are configured manually; Raaft does not document automated offer testing, traffic allocation or AI-generated copy.

Winback campaigns and revenue recovery

ProsperStack facilitates winback and dunning campaigns. Raaft focuses on the cancellation flow.

Raaft’s Customers At-risk list flags subscribers with missed payments or recent cancel-flow activity so your team can follow up, but Raaft does not publish dunning communications, payment retries or winback campaigns.

ProsperStack addresses passive churn through targeted dunning communications, secure payment updates and personalized recovery offers on supported billing platforms. It also connects cancellation context to winback campaigns and provides conversion incentives.

Which platform is the better fit?

Choose ProsperStack when:

  • Your cancellation volume and retained value justify an ongoing optimization program.
  • A retention, growth, product or subscription team will actively operate the system.
  • Your strategy benefits from CRM/CDP, behavioral or custom customer data.
  • You want to A/B test flows and offers and optimize AI-selected offers against explicit business economics.
  • You want winback, dunning and conversion campaigns alongside your cancellation flow.
  • You operate in SaaS, digital media or an ecommerce subscription model.

Consider Raaft when:

  • You run an early-stage or low-volume SaaS business adding its first cancellation flow.
  • You want a free plan, or unlimited sessions at a flat $299 per month.
  • Audience-based flows and conditional offers cover your needs without A/B testing.
  • Your billing runs on Stripe, Braintree, Recurly, Chargebee, Paddle or Lemon Squeezy.

ProsperStack vs. Raaft FAQs

What is the main difference between ProsperStack and Raaft?

The primary difference is product depth. Raaft emphasizes a simple, self-serve cancellation flow with conditional offers and exit feedback for SaaS teams. ProsperStack emphasizes the customer context, control, experimentation and measurement required to operate a sophisticated retention system anchored at cancellation.

Both products offer overlapping cancellation features, so the best choice depends on where your organization needs the most depth.

Is ProsperStack a good Raaft alternative?

Yes. ProsperStack can replace Raaft for businesses that need cancellation flows, targeted offers, segmentation and feedback analysis, and adds A/B testing, AI-optimized offers, CRM/CDP integrations, winback campaigns and dunning communications.

Which product is better for high-volume subscription businesses?

ProsperStack. Raaft’s Premium plan includes unlimited sessions, but it does not document A/B testing, weighted rollouts or automated offer optimization. ProsperStack is especially well suited to organizations that need advanced customer segmentation, controlled experiments and intend to continuously iterate and improve retention strategies.

Which company provides the best support?

Raaft offers chat support and setup advice for building flows. ProsperStack’s customer success team helps you implement and improve your retention strategy, and Enterprise plans add guided onboarding, dedicated developer support and one-on-one retention strategy sessions. In our experience, high-volume subscription companies building retention systems benefit from expert guidance and support. ProsperStack strives to be a partner, not just a vendor.

How does ProsperStack Autopilot compare to Raaft’s offers?

ProsperStack Autopilot automatically tests incentive combinations and prioritizes stronger performers. It can optimize acceptance or forecasted value, accepts gross margin and generosity constraints, generates offer copy and requires approval before AI-generated copy goes live.

Raaft presents the offer you configure for each cancellation reason or audience. It does not document an automated optimization equivalent.

How do ProsperStack’s payment recovery capabilities compare to Raaft?

ProsperStack provides targeted dunning communications, secure card updates and personalized recovery offers on supported billing platforms. Raaft flags subscribers with missed payments in its Customers At-risk list but does not publish dunning, payment retry or recovery features.

Which billing platforms do ProsperStack and Raaft support?

ProsperStack integrates with a broad list of SaaS, digital media and ecommerce subscription billing platforms, including Stripe, Chargebee, Recurly, Braintree, Maxio, Paddle, Recharge, Bold, Ordergroove, Naviga and others. Feature support varies by platform, and custom integration is available.

Raaft supports Stripe, Braintree, Recurly, Chargebee, Paddle and Lemon Squeezy.

Both companies price by monthly cancellation sessions. Raaft lists Free at $0 for 20 sessions, Basic at $79 for 100 sessions with $3 per additional session, and Premium at $299 for unlimited sessions. Can I migrate from Raaft to ProsperStack?

Yes, but migration effort will depend on your billing platform, audiences, offers, data sources and feature usage. Your migration plan should include documentation of existing flows and interventions, validation of billing actions in test mode and a controlled cutover.

Do both products support MCP and AI agents?

No. ProsperStack’s hosted MCP server lets supported AI tools such as Claude and ChatGPT read retention data and create or update flows, offers, segments and other resources using the connected user’s access. MCP is included on Prosper and Enterprise plans.

Raaft does not document MCP or AI agent access; it connects to other tools through Zapier and webhooks.

Which product is better for SaaS, digital media or ecommerce?

Raaft’s focus is SaaS. ProsperStack serves high-volume SaaS, digital media and ecommerce subscription businesses, including Dollar Shave Club, Nutrafol, Hootsuite, The Minnesota Star Tribune, SimplyCook and Roll20.
