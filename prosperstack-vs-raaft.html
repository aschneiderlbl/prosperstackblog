Overlapping tools, different retention priorities

For subscription teams deciding how much control they need over their cancellation program.

ProsperStack is a Raaft alternative for teams that want to build an ongoing cancellation optimization program using customer context, offer experimentation, and economic objectives. Raaft deserves evaluation when its cancellation flows, conditional offers, feedback, and reporting cover the work you need at its published price.

Both products address the cancellation moment. The decision is whether your team can execute its next retention test with the data, controls, and measurement available in the proposed plan.

We publish this comparison as the team behind ProsperStack. The recommendations reflect our view of fit, supported by current public documentation.

The retention optimization loop

1. Monitor churn with customer context

ProsperStack links cancellation feedback with billing, CRM/CDP, behavioral, and custom customer data. Its published integrations include Salesforce, HubSpot, Klaviyo, and Segment. Explore integrations.

Raaft collects cancellation feedback and publishes reporting for flow performance and reasons. Use your own segment definition to test whether the proposed setup supplies the attributes and reporting you need. See Raaft features.

For example, a SaaS team may want to distinguish customers who have not activated from customers who use the product but object to price. The useful question is whether the operator can make that distinction at cancellation and inspect the outcomes afterward.

2. Create a relevant intervention

ProsperStack lets the team tailor the experience, messaging, and offer to subscriber context. Supported subscription actions depend on the billing integration. Explore cancellation flows.

Raaft's configurable flow steps and conditional offers provide a way to respond to the cancellation reason. Compare the complete experience, including a customer who declines the offer and continues to cancel. See how Raaft flows work.

Do not default to a discount for every reason. A customer who no longer needs the service may not benefit from a cheaper version of it. Use the flow to learn what can be addressed and allow the rest of the feedback to inform product and lifecycle work.

3. Test, measure, and improve

ProsperStack supports flow and offer experiments. For a Raaft evaluation, demonstrate the specific comparison your team plans to run and how allocation and results would be managed.

Start with one segment and one intervention. Compare it with the existing approach, then inspect continued subscription activity, subsequent payments, and margin alongside acceptance. A lower software price may make Raaft the right choice if it supports the program you can operate today.

Autopilot and conditional offers

Conditional targeting determines which customers qualify for an intervention. Experimental optimization helps determine which variant should receive traffic. A retention program may need both.

ProsperStack Autopilot generates and tests incentive and copy variants within discount and duration limits. Operators approve generated copy before publication and choose acceptance or forecasted value as the objective. The value model considers discount cost, gross margin, and expected subsequent payments. Autopilot is included on Prosper and Enterprise plans. Explore Autopilot.

Raaft documents conditional offers and audience-selected flows. If automated allocation or an economic optimization objective is a requirement, ask for that exact demonstration. Those requirements should not be inferred from conditional targeting, and an unverified capability should not be labeled absent.

Before paying for more automation, identify who will approve the offer, review the results, and decide what to change. A tool's capability creates value only when the team uses it to improve an actual program.

Winback campaigns and revenue recovery

ProsperStack offers automated winback sequences based on subscription and cancellation context. Revenue Recovery supports dunning communications, secure payment updates, and recovery incentives alongside billing-platform retries. Explore Winback Campaigns and Revenue Recovery.

If you are evaluating Raaft primarily for cancellation flows, scope those adjacent requirements separately. Bring the required post-cancellation message, reactivation action, and recovery workflow to the vendor rather than assuming a cancellation integration covers them.

Which platform is the better fit?

Choose ProsperStack when

  • You need retention tooling that connects with your billing, customer-data, lifecycle, and AI stack across supported platforms.
  • You want your AI assistant to investigate cancellation data and help implement changes under your oversight.
  • Your team needs an ongoing program of cancellation experiments.
  • CRM/CDP, behavioral, or custom data is important to targeting.
  • You want to evaluate automated incentive selection against margin and expected value.
  • You want cancellation context to inform winback and recovery work.

Consider Raaft when

  • Its documented flows, offers, audience selection, and reporting meet your requirements.
  • Its published pricing fits the scale and scope of the program.
  • Your immediate task is improving a cancellation flow without adding requirements your team will not use.

Use a real cancellation scenario to test fit. A more extensive platform is worthwhile only when it enables work your team needs and will operate.

ProsperStack vs. Raaft FAQs

Is ProsperStack a Raaft alternative?

Yes. Both address subscription cancellation. Compare the data, interventions, experiments, and economics needed for your program.

Does Raaft support conditional cancellation experiences?

Yes. It documents conditional offers and multiple active flows selected by audience. Do not treat those capabilities as exclusive to ProsperStack.

When should we evaluate Autopilot?

When your team wants to automate incentive and copy testing within defined limits. Establish the objective and approval owner before enabling it.

Which integrations should we evaluate?

Start with the subscription platform, the customer attributes needed for targeting, and the downstream lifecycle action. ProsperStack publishes connections across SaaS, ecommerce, and publishing, plus CRM/CDP and automation tools. Ask for the complete workflow on your proposed plan, not just confirmation that a connector exists.

How can MCP help our retention team?

Use a compatible assistant to investigate cancellation data and help configure a test in ProsperStack. Access follows the connected user's permissions, and your team owns strategy and approval. MCP is available on Prosper and Enterprise plans; it does not establish that a proposed intervention will improve retention.

How does pricing compare?

Raaft lists Free at $0 for 20 monthly sessions, Basic at $79 for 100 sessions with $3 per additional session, and Premium at $299 for unlimited sessions. ProsperStack Grow starts at $200, while Prosper starts at $750 with Autopilot, CRM/CDP connections, custom targeting, and MCP. Raaft pricing and ProsperStack pricing.

Are cancellation sessions counted the same way?

Do not assume so. Raaft defines a session by unique user, outcome, and day. Compare each vendor's billing definition against your actual cancellation activity before estimating cost. See Raaft's session definition.

What should we verify before switching?

Rebuild one important flow and check its targeting, offers, billing actions, completed cancellation path, and reporting. Coordinate the cutover with any follow-up campaigns to avoid duplicate messages.
