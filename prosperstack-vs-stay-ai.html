The retention optimization loop

1. Monitor cancellation with customer context

For a physical-product subscription, the same cancellation reason can call for different responses. A customer with surplus product may need a later shipment, a longer recurring interval, or a different product. A delay changes the next shipment; a frequency change adjusts the recurring schedule.

ProsperStack combines cancellation feedback with billing, CRM/CDP, behavioral, and custom data. Its published integrations include Stay AI. Confirm the attributes and subscription actions available for your proposed implementation. Explore the Stay AI integration.

Bring an actual segment and cancellation reason to both demos. Inspect what the operator can target, what the subscriber sees, and where the result is reported.

2. Create an intervention that matches the reason

Stay AI's survey documentation lists actions such as changing frequency, delaying an order, applying a discount, offering a gift, swapping products, and using a custom URL. Those options are relevant to ecommerce subscription operations. See Stay AI's save-offer options.

ProsperStack lets your team configure targeted experiences and offers. Its broader business-model experience can provide ideas for the program, but each idea still needs to fit your products, supported billing actions, and customer behavior. Explore cancellation flows.

For surplus product, test a timing adjustment before assuming a discount is the answer. Check whether the subscriber completes the next order after accepting the change.

3. Test, measure, and improve

Both products support cancellation experiments. The question is whether your team can run the required comparison and evaluate downstream orders, revenue, and margin.

Use one segment and one intervention to begin. Keep the observation period consistent, and check repeat ordering alongside acceptance. A customer who accepts a delay has not yet demonstrated that the subscription will continue after the delay ends.

Which platform is the better fit?

Choose ProsperStack when

  • You need retention tooling that connects with your billing, customer-data, lifecycle, and AI stack across supported platforms.
  • You want your AI assistant to investigate cancellation data and help implement changes under your oversight.
  • You want a dedicated retention layer alongside your existing subscription platform.
  • You want to test incentives using explicit margin and value assumptions.
  • CRM/CDP or custom context is central to the targeting brief.
  • You want to draw on retention practices across business models and test their relevance to your ecommerce program.

Consider Stay AI when

  • You are choosing Shopify subscription infrastructure.
  • Its native cancellation actions and testing meet your retention requirements.
  • You want to operate subscriptions and the documented recovery and winback tools in one platform.

If the native tools already support your program, adding another layer needs a specific operational benefit. Identify the test, data, or control you are missing before expanding the stack.

ProsperStack vs. Stay AI FAQs

Does ProsperStack replace Stay AI subscription management?

No. This comparison concerns retention optimization. You still need a platform to manage the underlying subscription and billing operations.

Can ProsperStack work with Stay AI?

ProsperStack publishes a Stay AI integration. Confirm the required data and actions in your implementation before choosing which cancellation experience will run.

Does Stay AI support cancellation testing?

Yes. The current survey documentation includes manual A/B allocation and DecisionEngine. Evaluate the control and measurement your specific program needs.

Why consider cross-industry experience?

It can suggest new hypotheses about targeting, message design, and offer economics. It does not establish that an intervention successful in SaaS or publishing will work for physical-product subscriptions.

Which integrations should we evaluate?

Start with the subscription platform, the customer attributes needed for targeting, and the downstream lifecycle action. ProsperStack publishes connections across SaaS, ecommerce, and publishing, plus CRM/CDP and automation tools. Ask for the complete workflow on your proposed plan, not just confirmation that a connector exists.

How can MCP help our retention team?

Use a compatible assistant to investigate cancellation data and help configure a test in ProsperStack. Access follows the connected user's permissions, and your team owns strategy and approval. MCP is available on Prosper and Enterprise plans; it does not establish that a proposed intervention will improve retention.

How does pricing compare?

ProsperStack Grow starts at $200 per month, and Prosper starts at $750 with Autopilot, CRM/CDP connections, custom targeting, and MCP. Stay AI lists Pro at $499 per month plus 1% and $0.19 per transaction. The scopes and usage bases differ, so compare the total proposed setup. ProsperStack pricing and Stay AI pricing.

What should we check before adding ProsperStack?

Verify the subscriber attributes, subscription actions, customer-facing path, and ownership of winback and recovery messages. Demonstrate one test before scheduling the cutover.
