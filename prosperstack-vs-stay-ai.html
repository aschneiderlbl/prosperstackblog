Similar features, different approaches to retention

Stay AI is first and foremost a Shopify subscription platform, with cancellation surveys, winbacks and payment recovery as part of its broader offering. ProsperStack focuses on the cancellation experience, helping subscription businesses reduce churn through personalized flows, targeted offers and experimentation.

ProsperStack is designed for high-volume subscription businesses across SaaS, digital media and ecommerce that prioritize retention system depth and continuous improvement at scale. ProsperStack integrates with Shopify and works with the subscription platform you already use, including Stay AI.

ProsperStack vs. Stay AI, at a glance

Both ProsperStack and Stay AI are credible options for subscription businesses. The right choice depends on your subscription stack, volume, where you want depth and how your team thinks about retention.

Consider Stay AI if: You sell subscriptions on Shopify and want subscription management and retention tools in one platform. Its cancellation survey, WinbackEngine and Smart Dunning are included on every plan.

Choose ProsperStack if: You have high cancellation volume and want to systematically improve how you respond to churn. ProsperStack provides the customer context, conditional logic, experimentation, reporting and AI controls required to practice retention at the highest level.

Stay AI provides retention tools within its Shopify subscription platform.

ProsperStack is organized around retention as an ongoing practice, helping businesses develop retention strategies, deliver tailored interventions, test hypotheses, measure outcomes and continuously improve retention performance.

The retention optimization loop

Cancellation is one of the highest leverage moments in the customer lifecycle, and the greater a business's subscription churn volume, the greater the return on each improvement, big and small. Both platforms support customer segmentation, conditional experiences and AI experimentation. ProsperStack is built for the depth and customizability that high-volume brands need.

1. Monitor churn with greater context

Understanding cancellation and retention behavior at scale requires context. Stay AI's cancellation survey branches on subscription data such as churn risk, lifetime value, product, order number, shipping location and saved segments. ProsperStack imports Stay AI subscriber, subscription and plan properties and adds CRM/CDP properties and segments through native integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, Intercom, Klaviyo, Segment and Yotpo, providing you a clearer view of who is leaving, what they have in common and which churn patterns merit attention. Explore the Stay AI integration.

Additionally, connect your AI agent via MCP to analyze retention performance, suggest improvements and build them into your flow under your supervision. Stay AI's MCP connects subscription data to Claude for analysis and cannot make changes.

2. Create tailored interventions with more flexibility

Survey questions, offers and deflections are the main retention drivers at the cancellation moment. Stay AI's survey maps save offers to cancellation reasons: change frequency, delay or pause, discount code, gift, product swap, change address and custom URL. See Stay AI's save offers.

Through its Stay AI integration, ProsperStack applies coupons, product swaps, order reschedules, pauses and frequency changes. It also allows you to conditionally adapt flow routes, steps, survey questions, offers, deflections, confirmation pages and eligibility rules based on customer segments. Explore cancellation flows.

3. Test, measure and improve

Both platforms provide A/B testing options for flows and offers. Stay AI adds A/B testing nodes after a cancellation reason and can split traffic across surveys. Weighted rules in ProsperStack allow high-volume brands to validate changes on a smaller share of traffic before rolling out more broadly.

ProsperStack's AI Autopilot accelerates offer optimization by generating offer variants within your economic guardrails, testing them with customers and shifting traffic to the best performers. By factoring in discount amount, gross margin and post-save payments, Autopilot can optimize for long-term value, not just save rate.

Dashboards and reporting in ProsperStack show you where you're getting results, and where things could improve. Adding publication notes when saving a cancellation flow in ProsperStack allows you to see what effect each change had on performance.

Autopilot vs. DecisionEngine

Use AI to optimize offer performance in ProsperStack and Stay AI. Both systems direct more traffic toward stronger performers. They differ in what is tested and what the system optimizes for.

ProsperStack Autopilot

With Autopilot, you can:

  • Incorporate gross margin and expected post-offer payments to optimize for forecasted value, not just acceptance rate
  • Define discount, duration and maximum generosity boundaries
  • Generate and test offer headlines, body copy and button text
  • Review, edit or regenerate AI-created copy before it goes live
  • Test Autopilot against your existing offer to measure its impact

Autopilot handles ongoing variant generation, testing and allocation within those boundaries. The retention team still defines the objective and controls what the system is allowed to do. Explore Autopilot.

Stay AI DecisionEngine

DecisionEngine is a routing option on Stay AI's A/B testing nodes. Your team builds the save offers or cancellation screens to compare, and DecisionEngine uses machine learning to route traffic toward the best-performing one. Teams can set traffic percentages manually instead. See Stay AI's A/B testing nodes.

Winback campaigns and revenue recovery

Both platforms address what happens beyond the cancellation flow.

Stay AI's WinbackEngine learns the best time to re-engage churned subscribers and passes an event to Klaviyo to trigger your winback flow. Smart Dunning uses AI to set billing retry schedules after failed payments. Explore Stay AI churn prevention.

If running subscription management, payment retries and retention with one vendor on Shopify is your priority, Stay AI may be the stronger fit.

ProsperStack addresses passive churn through targeted dunning communications, secure payment updates and personalized recovery offers on supported billing platforms. It also connects cancellation context to winback campaigns and provides conversion incentives. Because ProsperStack integrates with Stay AI, you can add it without changing subscription platforms, and Smart Dunning continues to handle retries.

Which platform is the better fit?

Choose ProsperStack when:

  • Your cancellation volume and retained value justify an ongoing optimization program.
  • A retention, growth, product or subscription team will actively operate the system.
  • Your strategy benefits from CRM/CDP, behavioral or custom customer data.
  • Different customer segments require materially different experiences.
  • You want to test hypotheses and optimize AI-selected offers against explicit business economics.
  • You operate in SaaS, digital media or an ecommerce subscription model.

Consider Stay AI when:

  • You are choosing a Shopify subscription platform and want retention tools included.
  • Its cancellation survey, save offers and DecisionEngine meet your targeting and testing requirements.
  • You want AI-timed winbacks through Klaviyo and Smart Dunning for payment retries.
  • Running subscriptions and retention in one platform is more important than building a deeply operated cancellation program.

ProsperStack vs. Stay AI FAQs

What is the main difference between ProsperStack and Stay AI?

The primary difference is product emphasis. Stay AI is a Shopify subscription platform with native retention tools as part of its broader subscription offering. ProsperStack emphasizes the customer context, control, experimentation and measurement required to operate a sophisticated retention system anchored at cancellation.

Both products offer overlapping features, so the best choice depends on where your organization needs the most depth.

Is ProsperStack a good Stay AI alternative?

ProsperStack is an alternative to Stay AI's cancellation survey for businesses that need cancellation flows, targeted offers, segmentation, experimentation, feedback analysis and winback campaigns. ProsperStack is a retention layer, not a subscription platform, and it works alongside Stay AI through a native integration. Stay AI remains the subscription system of record.

Which product is better for high-volume subscription businesses?

Both products serve established subscription businesses. ProsperStack is especially well suited to organizations that need advanced customer segmentation, controlled experiments and intend to continuously iterate and improve retention strategies.

Which company provides the best support?

ProsperStack provides a dedicated account manager to help you implement and improve your retention strategy. Stay AI also includes a dedicated account manager and support team on every plan. In our experience, high-volume subscription companies building retention systems benefit from expert guidance and support. ProsperStack strives to be a partner, not just a vendor.

How do ProsperStack Autopilot and Stay AI DecisionEngine differ?

Both systems use AI to direct more traffic toward stronger performers. ProsperStack Autopilot can optimize acceptance or forecasted value, accepts gross margin and generosity constraints, generates offer copy and requires approval before AI-generated copy goes live.

Stay AI DecisionEngine routes traffic between the save offers or cancellation screens your team builds.

How does ProsperStack's payment recovery capabilities compare to Stay AI?

ProsperStack provides targeted dunning communications, secure card updates and personalized recovery offers on supported billing platforms. It works alongside your platform's retry logic and does not retry payments itself.

Stay AI's Smart Dunning uses AI to set retry schedules after billing failures. It keeps that role when you add ProsperStack.

Which billing platforms do ProsperStack and Stay AI support?

ProsperStack integrates with a broad list of SaaS, digital media and ecommerce subscription billing platforms, including Stripe, Chargebee, Recurly, Braintree, Maxio, Paddle, Recharge, Bold, Ordergroove, Stay AI, Naviga and others. Feature support varies by platform, and custom integration is available.

Stay AI is itself a subscription platform, built for Shopify.

How does ProsperStack pricing compare with Stay AI?

ProsperStack is priced by monthly cancellation session volume. Grow starts at $200 per month and Prosper starts at $750 per month, with custom Enterprise pricing. See ProsperStack pricing.

Stay AI's Pro plan is $499 per month plus 1% and 19¢ per transaction, with all features included. Enterprise pricing is custom. That price covers subscription management as well as retention, so the two are not like for like. See Stay AI pricing.

Can I use ProsperStack with Stay AI?

Yes. Connect Stay AI with an API key, authorize Shopify and add the ProsperStack snippet to your active Shopify theme. ProsperStack then opens from the cancellation action in the Stay AI customer portal, and accepted offers are applied in Stay AI. Your plan should include documentation of existing survey paths and save offers, validation of subscription actions in test mode and a controlled cutover. Explore the Stay AI integration.

Do both products support MCP and AI agents?

Both companies offer MCP access, at different levels.

ProsperStack's hosted MCP server lets supported AI tools read retention data and create or update flows, offers, segments and other resources using the connected user's access.

Stay AI's MCP connects subscription data to Claude for analysis. It cannot make changes to subscriptions or settings.

Which product is better for SaaS, digital media or ecommerce?

Stay AI's focus is Shopify ecommerce subscription brands. ProsperStack serves high-volume SaaS, digital media and ecommerce subscription businesses, including Dollar Shave Club, Nutrafol, Hootsuite, The Minnesota Star Tribune, SimplyCook and Roll20.
