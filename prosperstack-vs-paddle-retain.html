Overlapping tools, different retention priorities

Paddle Billing includes Retain cancellation flows at no additional cost. Add ProsperStack when a dedicated cancellation program needs more connected context and operating control.

Paddle Billing customers already have a meaningful native retention option. Retain cancellation flows can collect feedback and present salvage offers, with accepted actions handled through Paddle. The native customer portal integration makes those flows available without an additional Retain cancellation-flow charge.

The reason to evaluate ProsperStack is a specific program requirement: how you want to personalize cancellation, run experiments, connect customer context, and operate the work. You keep Paddle as the merchant of record and billing platform.

This comparison is for SaaS and digital subscription teams evaluating native Paddle Billing retention against a dedicated layer. ProsperStack publishes it and is one of the providers discussed. Our churn management software guide provides the broader category context.

The retention optimization loop

Give the included native flow a fair test

Paddle's cancellation-flow documentation describes surveys and salvage attempts such as discounts, pauses, plan switches, and support. With Paddle Billing, accepted actions can be applied automatically.

The January 2026 customer portal release brought that experience into the native portal at no additional cost for Paddle Billing customers. If its offers and experience meet your requirements, there is a strong case for using what is already available.

Retain also has options for other billing stacks. The native implementation discussed here is specifically Paddle Billing; compare the implementation path separately if you use another platform.

Add a retention layer for a defined operating need

ProsperStack lets your team use subscription context and cancellation responses to shape conditional experiences, compare tests, and carry the learning into winback. The Paddle integration connects that work to the billing record.

For example, a customer with a recent support escalation may need a support path, while an established customer citing price may be eligible for an offer test. Connected customer data helps your team make that distinction rather than treating every cancellation as the same discount opportunity.

Before adding a platform, write down the difference you need from the current experience. Ask both vendors to demonstrate that flow with your intended data. A separate subscription is justified by the work it enables, not by having a longer feature list.

Connect retention to the tools your team already uses

A cancellation decision often depends on more than the subscription record. A customer may have an unresolved support issue, low product usage, or a recent lifecycle campaign that changes which intervention makes sense.

ProsperStack's integration ecosystem connects subscription platforms across SaaS, ecommerce, and publishing, including Stripe, Recurly, Paddle, Recharge, Stay AI, Piano, and Naviga. Connections with Salesforce, HubSpot, Segment, and Klaviyo bring customer and lifecycle context into the same retention practice. CRM and CDP integrations are available on Prosper and Enterprise.

The useful comparison is which tools can supply context to your cancellation program and act on what you learn. Map the billing record, customer attributes, and follow-up channel your first test needs. Confirm those connections before buying. This breadth is useful for businesses with several subscription systems.

Measure the customer relationship after the offer

Start with one segment and one hypothesis. Compare the experience with a suitable control, then follow accepted offers through subsequent renewals. Check retained revenue, discount cost, and whether customers cancel again.

A higher acceptance rate can be encouraging without establishing incremental retained value. Use the downstream outcome to decide whether to keep, change, or expand the test.

Make the offer relevant to the subscription

Soundstripe's case study describes segmenting subscribers by plan and testing plan-specific coupons. That is a useful question for a Paddle team: does the offer fit the package the customer actually has?

The example demonstrates segmentation and testing, not an expected result for your Paddle program. Start with the relevant plan and measure what happens after the offer.

Use MCP to move from a retention question to a test

Paddle MCP supports Paddle API operations with permission controls. OAuth connections start with read access by default; authorized operations depend on the connection or API-key scope. Compare the billing actions you need with ProsperStack's retention configuration workflow.

ProsperStack's MCP Server connects compatible AI assistants to cancellation data and retention configuration. You can investigate a change in cancellation reasons, compare segments, and have the assistant help create or update a flow, offer, or segment.

For example, ask which established subscribers cite price, review their offer history, then prepare a test for that audience. A person still decides the audience, offer economics, and approved changes. Access follows the connected user's permissions. MCP is available on Prosper and Enterprise.

MCP helps with analysis and implementation. Autopilot, also on Prosper and Enterprise, serves a different purpose: optimizing eligible offers within your configured limits. Keep the test question and the decision to expand with your team.

Winback campaigns and revenue recovery

Keep Paddle Payment Recovery running

ProsperStack's Paddle integration does not replace Retain Payment Recovery. Paddle continues to handle its failed-payment recovery program.

That distinction matters when choosing an alternative. A voluntary cancellation experiment can run alongside native recovery, while payment retries and dunning remain with Paddle. Keep the commercial and implementation discussion focused on the cancellation program you are changing.

Which platform is the better fit?

Choose ProsperStack when:

  • Your cancellation volume and retained value justify an ongoing optimization program.
  • A retention, growth, product, or subscription team will operate and improve the experience.
  • Your strategy needs connected customer context and different paths for different segments.
  • You want to test offers and use MCP to investigate retention data and prepare approved changes.
  • You want to keep Paddle billing while giving cancellation optimization its own operating home.

Consider Paddle Retain when:

  • The native surveys and salvage offers meet your current needs.
  • You want accepted actions handled directly through Paddle Billing.
  • You have no defined requirement that justifies a separate paid retention layer.

ProsperStack vs. Paddle Retain FAQs

Is Paddle Retain only payment recovery?

No. Retain also provides cancellation flows and surveys. Its native Paddle Billing implementation can automatically handle accepted salvage actions.

Are cancellation flows included with Paddle Billing?

Paddle's customer portal release describes them as available at no additional cost to Paddle Billing customers. Confirm current terms for your account.

Does ProsperStack replace Paddle as merchant of record?

No. Paddle remains the merchant of record and billing platform.

Does ProsperStack replace Retain Payment Recovery?

No. The Paddle integration page explicitly keeps Retain Payment Recovery in place.

Can Retain work with other billing platforms?

Paddle markets Retain cancellation flows for existing billing stacks. Implementation and action handling can differ from native Paddle Billing.

How should we start with ProsperStack?

Connect Paddle and integrate the cancellation experience into your site. Work with customer success to select the first segment and offer, confirming the supported Paddle action before launch.
