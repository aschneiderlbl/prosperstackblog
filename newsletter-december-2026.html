Why do customers leave, and what could give them a reason to stay?

We’ve been digging into cancellation feedback and retention offers across our SaaS and ecommerce samples. This month, we’re sharing two reports to help you choose what to test in 2027.

01 / SaaS retention trends

A discount can’t solve every reason for leaving.

Some customers haven’t built a product habit. Some say they’re getting answers from AI tools instead. Others have reached the end of a project, or their needs have changed.

Our SaaS report explores these different situations and ideas to test in 2027, from helping customers build a routine to showing them a relevant next use for the product.

A question for your team:
Does your cancellation experience address why the customer wants to leave?

02 / Ecommerce retention trends

More accepted offers don’t always mean more lasting value.

Our ecommerce findings look at cancellation reasons, temporary breaks, and what happened after customers accepted a discount.

In the randomized examples studied, deeper discounts won more acceptances, but the additional recorded revenue varied. The report also explores why a temporary break deserves a different response from price concerns or a product backlog.

A question for your team:
What happens 60 to 90 days after a customer accepts your offer?

Put the findings to work

Choose one reason. Plan one test.

Start with a cancellation reason that matters to your business. Choose an alternative that fits that reason, then follow what customers do next: use the product, make another payment, or cancel later.

These reports describe the ProsperStack samples studied, rather than industry-wide benchmarks. The SaaS observations include consumer subscription apps and business software. See each report for its evidence and limitations.
