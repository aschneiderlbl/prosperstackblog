01 / The retention idea

Look beyond the initial save.

A customer accepting an offer is a useful signal. What happens afterward matters, too.

Our recent ecommerce research found that deeper discounts won more acceptances, but didn’t always deliver much more recorded revenue over the following 90 days.

For your next test, track:
Initial acceptance · Subsequent payments
Continued subscriptions · Cost of the offer

Choose one offer to review before expanding it. Is it helping customers stay, or simply delaying their cancellation?
